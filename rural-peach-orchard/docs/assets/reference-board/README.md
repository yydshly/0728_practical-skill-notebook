## Use policy

Most entries are linked references. The one stored image is the project-owned
generated concept identified below. Every external URL was opened and checked
on 2026-07-31 for a concrete image or image gallery and an attributable
publisher/author. `shape-reference-only` means the image may inform
proportions, pose, construction, color families, or wear placement, but its
pixels must not be copied, projected, traced into a texture, included in an
asset, or used to train a project-specific model. `generated-2d-reference-only`
applies the same production restriction to the stored concept board.

Where a source page exposes several gallery frames, the `File` column names the
frame's production purpose. Manufacturer and government pages do not grant
asset-reuse rights here; they remain shape reference only. Wikimedia license
labels are recorded for auditability even though this project is not reusing
the image pixels.

## Direction extracted from the board

- House: use the low, horizontally layered massing and mixed old/new repair
  language of North China, while simplifying any ornamental historic detail.
- Vehicle: use the dimensional pages to set scale; no single manufacturer's
  styling, logo, grille pattern, or exact panel design may be reproduced.
- Tree: the harvest version must replace bloom with leaf/fruit density. Keep
  rows open enough for the specified tricycle and avoid vineyard-like trellises.
- Character: retain contemporary Chinese orchard clothing and sun protection.
  For crate motion, prioritize the NIOSH posture evidence over incidental unsafe
  postures visible in documentary photographs.
- Crates: the contemporary Mengyin production reference controls form and
  workflow. Use reusable ventilated plastic crates with integral handholds and
  stable stacking rims. The 1940 Colorado image controls load density and stack
  relationship only; its wooden slats, material, joinery and period character
  are prohibited.
