The production `.blend`, four 256 px base-color textures and both delivered
GLBs are original Blender 4.5.12 authored content. The mesh was built from
explicit surface cages, then skinned, weighted and animated specifically for
this project. The textures are deterministic project-authored color/wear maps
generated by `tools/blender/build_farmer_character.py`; they contain no source
pixels from the concept. No MetaHuman, scan, marketplace base, paid asset,
downloaded rig, third-party mesh, texture, logo or branded clothing element is
included.

## Runtime collision policy

The source `COLLISION` collection and delivered `collision.glb` are
intentionally empty. Gameplay supplies a runtime capsule downstream. Suggested
starting sizes are 0.29 m radius × 1.62 m height standing, 0.27 × 1.50 m during
interaction, and 0.25 × 1.18 m seated in the vehicle; runtime tuning may adjust
these states without changing the authored character.
